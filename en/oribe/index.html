Stack the bowls. Let them fall.

For YouTube Playables, in development

In a village of Oribe potters, an apprentice stacks the bowls left in the kiln yard until they fall. A cat spirit is born from the way they break. With a band of cats, the apprentice works through twelve chapters of contests against the few people the god left moving.

  • Stack it, drop it, meet a catHow the pieces land on the shelf, and how they fall, decides which cat you get.
  • Two or three minutes a roundClose it whenever you like. It picks up where you left off.
  • Crooked on purposeOribe ware treats warped shapes as beautiful. The first colour to return to the grey world is Oribe green.
