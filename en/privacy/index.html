Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The site is served by GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colton Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes access data such as your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser type, to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a stable, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This data may be processed in the United States. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

We use no analytics, no cookies, and no external fonts or trackers. The pages load nothing from third-party servers.

Email

If you write to us, we use your name, email address and message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete them once the matter is settled and no retention duty applies.

Our YouTube publishing tool

We use the YouTube API Services in a small tool that uploads our devlog videos to our own channel, Haus Tacorari, and schedules when they go public.

  • We are its only users. It does not connect to anyone else's account.
  • It reads and writes data for the Haus Tacorari channel only: video uploads, titles, descriptions, publish times and thumbnails, plus a check of the channel name.
  • The sign-in token stays on our own work computer. We do not send it anywhere or share it.
  • We do not use any of this data for advertising, sell it, or pass it to third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in your Google Account security settings.

The YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply to this tool.
