Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This page explains how Haus Tacorari ("we") handles information on this website and in the tool we use to run our own YouTube channel.

This website

The site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added any analytics, cookies or third-party trackers, and we do not collect personal data from visitors. Anything GitHub records to serve the site is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Our YouTube publishing tool

We use the YouTube API Services in a small tool that uploads our devlog videos to our own channel, Haus Tacorari, and schedules when they go public.

  • We are its only users. It does not connect to anyone else's account.
  • It reads and writes data for the Haus Tacorari channel only: video uploads, titles, descriptions, publish times and thumbnails, plus a check of the channel name.
  • The sign-in token stays on our own work computer. We do not send it anywhere or share it.
  • We do not use any of this data for advertising, sell it, or pass it to third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because the tool uses the YouTube API Services, the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) also apply.
