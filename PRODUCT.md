# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with no build step (user decision, 2026-11-14). Published with GitHub Pages straight from the repository. Any JavaScript must be small and optional; every page reads correctly without it.

## Users

The main visitors are players. They arrive from the Haus Tacorari YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, or a shared link, usually on a phone, and want to know what each game is, what it looks like, and whether it is worth following.

Secondary visitors, served by the same pages without changing their priority: reviewers of the YouTube API audit, who need the privacy policy; press, publishers, and grant juries, who need the studio's contact address.

## Product Purpose

This is the studio site for Haus Tacorari. It introduces three games in development and links to the devlog channel. Success means a player can tell the three games apart within a few seconds and understand each one's world. The privacy policy must also live at a stable public URL.

## Positioning

Haus Tacorari films how its games are made, prompt by prompt, and says plainly what is AI work and what is handmade. The story text and the music of *Stella Is a Wolf* are the author's own.

## Operating Context

- New devlog episodes appear on YouTube on Tuesdays and Fridays (Stella), Sundays (Neko to Oribe), and Wednesdays (game news).
- The site is maintained by one person, sometimes with Claude. Pages must be easy to edit by hand.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two languages, Japanese and English, on separate pages with a JA/EN switch. Japanese is primary.
- Pages: home, Stella Is a Wolf (`stella`), Neko to Oribe (`oribe`), Tsukumogami Board (`tsukumogami`), privacy policy (`privacy`).
- English game titles are provisional.
- No analytics, cookies, or third-party trackers. The privacy policy says so, and the site must keep that true.
- Platforms: Stella is for PC. Neko to Oribe is for YouTube Playables. The Tsukumogami Board has no platform decided yet, so the site must not state one.

## Brand Commitments

- The channel logo assets are in `C:\Users\salon\Videos\stella-devlog\_channel\` (`logo_mark_1080.png`, `logo_wordmark.png`, `logo_wordmark_dark.png`).

## Product Principles

1. The games come first. Each one should feel like itself, not like a template.
2. Never claim what is not decided: no dates, prices, or platforms beyond the ones listed above.
3. Be honest about AI use, in the wording the user approved.
4. It must work on a phone, because most visitors come from vertical video.
